Work

Shops, clinics, schools and showrooms. Each one runs on its own numbers, so the work is judged by enquiries and sales, not by a launch.

Electric Studio

E-bike and e-moto shop, Poland

Shop theme, product data, Google Ads with Merchant Center and supplier integrations for a Shoper store selling Surron, Talaria and E Ride Pro.

Under the hood

  • GA4 and Tag Manager rebuilt: the purchase event fired on every page view and inflated sales 280 times. One trigger condition fixed it.
  • Supplier feeds and stock synced into the shop; product pages, instalment blocks and delivery dates generated from data.
  • Search campaigns split by model, exact-match exclusions for bare brand names, search partners off, location targeting by presence.

Measured

  • Return on ad spend checked against paid orders, not the panel: the real figure was one eighth of what the dashboard showed.
  • GA4 saw 68% of orders, the rest came in by phone. Since then every report carries two columns: measured and paid.

What I recommended

  • Budget follows verified paid orders only. A pre-order page for the model whose searches double every month.

LEVEL

Language school, three locations

Google Ads rebuilt around enquiries rather than clicks, SEO on Squarespace.

Under the hood

  • Search-term audit: eight of ten measured conversions came from people typing the school's own name. Generic terms produced none.
  • Three campaigns, one per intent, exclusion lists of 165 competitor names and other towns, a cost-per-click ceiling instead of automated bidding on too little data.

Measured

  • Clicks costing eight times the average stopped the day the ceiling went in. Cost per enquiry on the brand campaign is a fifth of the course campaign's.
  • Two of six calls from ads went unanswered in working hours, and the report says so out loud.

What I recommended

  • More budget on the campaign that loses half its impressions to budget, not on the one that buys clicks.

KIOKUYA

Own product: CRM for vehicle dealers

A complete back office for an electric vehicle dealer, running in production: customers, pre-orders, stock, service, invoices and marketing in one system that several companies can share without seeing each other's data.

Under the hood

  • Python, FastAPI and Postgres with row-level security separating companies inside the database; personal data encrypted with a separate key for every customer.
  • Pre-orders with deposits and a public status page where the buyer follows each stage; stock where a vehicle can be reserved before it even arrives; service orders on the same timeline.
  • Invoices with the vehicle identification number on every line and fleet invoices covering many vehicles; payment status read from the invoicing system through a webhook; sync with the Shoper store.
  • Marketing with a consent register: an imported contact is not a marketing consent. Email campaigns with bounce and unsubscribe handling, and a dashboard with vehicles to hand over and orders stuck at a stage.

KIODEM ADS

Own tool: Google Ads and SEO automation

A console that reviews every client's Google Ads and Search Console accounts on its own each week, points out what to fix and later checks whether the change actually worked.

Under the hood

  • Monday morning review of every account: impression share lost to budget and to rank, conversions, cost per conversion, search terms and Google changes from recent weeks.
  • Every decision goes into a log with its evidence, rule and check date; after 14 or 28 days the console compares before and after. More than 230 decisions so far.
  • A watchdog script inside each client account runs hourly on Google's servers and sends an email alert when something unusual happens.
  • Search Console joined with Google Ads shows terms paid for in ads that the site already ranks for organically. An account guard stops any script whose account ID does not match the client profile.

Measured

  • Language school: after the budget increase the review recommended, twice as many enquiry forms at the same cost per form.
  • E-moto shop: 43% more conversions in 30 days at an 8% lower cost per conversion.
  • The after-change check caught a phone-call conversion that Google had quietly added to bid optimisation, before it could skew the campaign's learning.
